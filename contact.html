Frequently Asked Questions

What's your delivery time?

We typically deliver within 30-45 minutes depending on location and restaurant preparation time.

Do you deliver to my area?

We currently serve all major areas in Lagos. Enter your address in our app to check if we deliver to you.

How can I cancel my order?

You can cancel orders within 5 minutes of placement through the app. After that, contact support immediately.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept debit/credit cards, bank transfers, mobile money (MTN Momo), and cash on delivery.

Is my order insured?

Yes! All orders are covered by our food protection guarantee. If there's an issue, we'll make it right.

How do I report a problem?

Contact our support team via this form, call us, or use the "Report Issue" button in your order tracking.
