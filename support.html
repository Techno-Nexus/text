Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery take?
Most orders are delivered within 30 minutes during peak hours and 15-20 minutes during off-peak hours. You can track your order in real-time.
What if my order arrives late?
If your order is late, you'll receive a discount on your next order. We also offer a full refund if it arrives after 45 minutes from confirmation.
Can I modify my order?
Yes, you can modify your order within 5 minutes of placing it. After that, you'll need to cancel and create a new order.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept cards (Visa, Mastercard), mobile transfers (Paystack, Flutterwave), and cash on delivery in selected areas.
