Our Story

FoodExpress was founded in 2020 with a simple mission: to make delicious food accessible to everyone. We started as a small team with big dreams and have grown into Lagos' most trusted food delivery platform.

Our journey began when our founder, Chisom, realized how difficult it was to find fresh, quality food delivered quickly. She decided to change that by building a platform that connects food lovers with the best restaurants and independent chefs.

Today, we serve over 50,000 customers across Lagos, deliver over 2,000 orders daily, and work with 200+ partner restaurants. Our commitment to quality, speed, and customer satisfaction remains unchanged.
